ACADEMIC CATALOG

Academic Exellence

at MSRI

Rooted in African indigenous Spirituality, delivering world-class qualifications
across Science, Law, Arts, and Commerce. Explore our comprehensive faculties
designed to bridge ancestral wisdom with modern academics.

Science & Health

Promoting ecological sustainability, public health, and animal welfare through modern
technological research grounded in traditional ecological knowledge.

Faculty of Science and Agriculture

Sustainable farming methods, climate resilience, and foundational scientific research tailored for regional food security and ecological conservation.

Health Sciences

Preventative healthcare, public health policy, and restorative therapies. Prepares students for diverse medical and therapeutic careers with a focus on holistic wellbeing.

Department of Animal Care

Ethical veterinary care, wildlife conservation, and regional animal husbandry practices. Programs support local biodiversity, ethical treatment, and community livelihoods.

Business, Law & Society

Developing ethical leaders with social, legal, and economic
perspectives to forge just communities and sustainable commerce.

Faculty of Business and Economic Studies

Sustainable commerce, ethical finance, social entrepreneurship, and market strategy. Programs are designed for modern venture creation and economic development.

Faculty of Law

Rigorous legal training focused on justice, human rights, and constitutional law. Prepares advocates for ethical public and private service across Africa.

Faculty of Social Science and Humanities

Exploring societal dynamics, behavioral psychology, and critical cultural thought. Programs cover psychology, sociology, political science, and history.

Arts, Media & Culture

Championing indigenous voices, preserving cultural heritage, and cultivating creative
innovation through traditional arts, media, and spirituality.

Faculty of Indigenous Spirituality and African Matters

Exploration of profound ancestral wisdom, cosmological frameworks, and holistic wellness paradigms to root identity and native cultural knowledge.

Faculty of Media and Journalism

Ethics-driven journalism, strategic communication, and digital content production. Prepares students to tell truthful, impactful, and socially conscious stories.

Faculty of Art and Music

Cultivating creative mastery, artistic expression, and cultural preservation. Programs range from traditional craftsmanship to contemporary performance and studio arts.

Tourism & Specialized

Fostering global connections, sustainable tourism, and career-ready technical skills
through world-class hospitality training and accredited specialized qualifications.

Faculty of Hospitalities and Tourism

Specialized management training for the global travel, event, and service industry, emphasizing sustainable tourism practices and cultural authenticity.

TC Entity Qualifications

Industry-recognized technical certificates and practical skill-based programs aligned with market demands, enabling rapid career entry and upskilling.
