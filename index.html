Our Foundation & Vision

The Mzuvukile Slabbert Radebe Institute (formerly African Centre of Excellence) was established in 2015 with a profound vision: to nurture Indigenous African thought and leadership.

Founded by HSRM Imboni Dr. uZwi-Lezwe Radebe, MSRI is dedicated to empowering individuals and organizations across Africa through innovative education, grounded in African Indigenous Spirituality and identity.
