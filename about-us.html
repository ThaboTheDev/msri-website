Our Heritage

Forging an African Centre of Excellence

Tracing our journey from a visionary concept in 2015 to a premier institution bridging indigenous wisdom with modern academics.

2015

The Founding Vision

The Mzuvukile Slabbert Radebe Institute (MSRI) was officially established in 2015 with a dedicated mission to empower individuals and organizations across the African continent. The institution was founded on the unique, pioneering philosophy of seamlessly integrating African Indigenous Spirituality into structured education, guided by the core recognition that "life itself is spiritual".

Institutional Growth

Expanding the Mission

As the institute grew, MSRI expanded its operational mission to deliver highly transformative learning experiences, actively support entrepreneurship, and drive lasting socioeconomic development. By forging strategic partnerships and launching impactful community initiatives, the institute positioned itself as a primary catalyst for sustainable growth.

Digital Era

Digital Transformation & LMS

To broaden its pan-African accessibility, MSRI successfully launched its comprehensive Learning Management System (MSRI LMS). This e-learning platform was designed to provide flexible, high-quality course content crafted by industry experts, empowering scholars to learn at their own pace while meticulously tracking their academic progress.

June 13, 2026

A New Era of QCTO Accreditation

MSRI celebrated a landmark milestone when the Quality Council for Trades and Occupations (QCTO) officially accredited three of its specialized skills programs. This prestigious event was attended by distinguished leadership, including His Spiritual Royal Majesty (HSRM) Imboni Dr., cementing MSRI's status as a legally recognized and spiritually grounded skills development provider.

Our Guiding Philosophy

The foundational pillars that shape our curriculum, drive our faculty, and empower our scholars across the continent.

Indigenous Spirituality

We recognize that life itself is spiritual. Our academic frameworks are deeply rooted in African heritage, ensuring scholars develop profound cultural identity alongside their professional skills.

Academic Excellence

Through rigorous DHET and QCTO compliance, we deliver world-class, industry-aligned training. We bridge the gap between ancestral wisdom and the demands of the modern commercial landscape.

Pan-African Empowerment

Our mission extends beyond the classroom. We are catalysts for socio-economic growth, forging strategic partnerships to foster entrepreneurship and ethical leadership across Africa.
